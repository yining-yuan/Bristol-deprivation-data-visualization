Sources & method

Every line is drawn from published, machine-readable data

Labour-market and pay series are pulled live from the Office for National Statistics via the Nomis API; education, life-expectancy and income series come from Department for Education, Office for National Statistics and Department for Work and Pensions releases. Each is stored in bristol_trends.csv before being drawn here. Where a national measure or definition changed mid-series (e.g. the early years foundation stage profile framework, or absence recording), it is noted on the card. "England average" is the benchmark line; some series compare against Great Britain or the UK where England is not published — see each source.
